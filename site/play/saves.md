# Saves on several devices

When the person who set up the game turned on **cloud save sync**, your saves follow your email: save on your phone, continue on your PC.

## How it works

- **When the game opens**, it compares this device's saves with the cloud and keeps the newer copy of each. A small ☁ message says *"Saves are in sync"* or how many saves it downloaded.
- **When you save**, the save uploads about a second later (*"☁ Saved to the cloud"*).
- **Offline**, you play with this device's saves. They upload once you are back online.

You don't need to do anything: save in the game as usual.

## Messages you may see

### "The game may be open on another device"

Another device saved in the last 5 minutes. If the game is still open there, close it, so the two devices don't overwrite each other's saves. Then tap **Got it**.

### "This device's saves differ from the cloud"

A save changed both here and in the cloud since the last sync, for example after playing on two devices while one was offline. The message lists each save with the time and device of the cloud copy. Choose:

| Choice | Keeps |
|---|---|
| **Use the cloud's** | The cloud copies (from your other device) |
| **Keep this device's** | This device's copies, and uploads them |

The choice applies to all the listed saves. If you are unsure, export the save you may lose first ([how](/play/save-files#export-a-save)), then choose.

### "The cloud has a newer save"

You just saved, but another device updated the same slot in the cloud. **Overwrite cloud** replaces it with the save you just made. **Later** keeps it on this device only; the next time the game opens, it asks which copy to keep ([see above](#this-device-s-saves-differ-from-the-cloud)).

### "Login expired"

Reload the page and sign in again. Saves made meanwhile are kept on this device and upload after the reload.

## Good habits

- Play on **one device at a time**, and let the game finish syncing (the ☁ message) before switching.
- Use the **same email** everywhere.
- Before something risky (a boss, a big choice), save in a **new slot** rather than over your only save.
