# Fast forward

Speeds up the whole game ×2, ×3 or ×4: walking, battles, animations, text and cutscenes. Music and sound keep their normal speed.

![The ⏩ ×2 button in the toolbar, next to ⚙](/img/controls-landscape.png){.shot .landscape}

| On | Turn on / off | Only while held |
|---|---|---|
| Phone or tablet | Tap **⏩** in the toolbar (it lights up while on) | Hold **⏩** |
| PC | <kbd>`</kbd> (the key left of 1) | Hold <kbd>Tab</kbd> |
| Anywhere | ⚙ → **Fast forward** | |

Pick the speed with ⚙ → **Fast-forward speed**. It is remembered in this browser; fast forward itself starts off each time.

The keys do nothing when one of the game's plugins already uses them. Fast forward stops while the ⚙ menu is open, and some sites have it turned off by the person who set up the game or by the game's creator.
