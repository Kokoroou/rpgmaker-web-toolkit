# Keys and buttons

In the menu: ⚙ → **Controls** → **Keys and buttons**.

Open ⚙ → **Controls** → **Keys and buttons** to choose which key or gamepad button does what. The game is paused while the panel is open.

1. Under **Keyboard** or **Gamepad**, tap **Change** next to an action: OK / confirm, Cancel (Esc also opens the menu), Dash, Previous page, Next page. The gamepad also has Menu.
2. Press the key, or the button on the gamepad. Press **Esc** (keyboard) or tap **Cancel** to leave it as it was.
3. **Reset to defaults** puts back the keys the game started with.

A new key **replaces** the action's old keys (OK is Enter, Space and Z by default; after you choose F it is only F). A key does one thing: if you give it to another action, that action loses it and shows *No key*, so change it or reset.

The arrows and F1 (which opens the ⚙ menu) cannot be changed. The on-screen buttons on a phone are not affected.

Your choices are kept in this browser, on this device, not with your saves: a keyboard and a gamepad differ from one device to the next. Some sites turn this off, or allow only the keyboard or only the gamepad.
