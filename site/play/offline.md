# Play offline

In the menu: ⚙ → **Play offline** (the **Offline** group, on the first screen).

Download the game to your device once, and it opens and plays without a connection. Saves you make offline stay on the device and upload when you are back online.

## Download the game

1. Open the game while online, preferably on Wi-Fi: the download can be hundreds of megabytes.
2. ⚙ → **Play offline** shows how many files and how much space it needs, and how much is free.
3. Tap **Download**. A bar shows the progress. You can close the panel and keep playing: the download goes on while the game is open. The screen stays on while it runs, where the browser allows it.

| What happens | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pause**, a closed tab, a dropped connection | Nothing is lost. Open **Play offline** again and tap **Continue**: files already on the device are not downloaded twice |
| *Not enough space* | The game needs more than the device has free. Free some space, or clear another site's data, and open the panel again |
| *You are signed out* | Your login expired. Reload the page, sign in and continue |
| A few files *did not download* | Tap **Continue** to try those again |

When the panel says **Everything is on this device**, turn on airplane mode and open the game's link: it starts from the files on the device. Without ⚙ → **Play offline**, the browser still keeps the files you played through, but not those of places you never reached.

## New version of the game

When the person who set up the game publishes a new version, the next launch (after every tab of the game is closed once) removes the files that changed. ⚙ → **Play offline** then shows how much is left to download. Open it while online and tap **Continue**: only the changed files download.

## Install it as an app

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| Android, Chrome, PC | ⚙ → **Play offline** → **Install app** (or the browser's install button in the address bar). The game then opens full screen from its own icon |
| iPhone / iPad (Safari) | Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Do this: Safari deletes a website's files after about a week without a visit, but not an app's |

An installed game keeps its downloaded files like the website does. Your saves and your login follow your email either way.

## Free the space

⚙ → **Help** → [Game files on this device](/play/help#game-files-on-this-device) shows what is kept and clears it. The game then downloads again as you play, or when you tap **Download**. Saves are not touched.

## Limits

- Over a connection that never reaches the server, the game opens but cloud saves wait until it does ([Cloud saves](/play/saves)).
- **Sign in once while online** after the login expires, even on an installed copy.
- Some sites have this turned off by the person who set up the game, or by the game's creator.
