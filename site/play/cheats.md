# Cheats

⚙ → **Cheats** opens a panel to change the game as you play. It is there only when the person who set up the game [turned cheats on](/setup/configuration#common-changes), for everyone or just for you; the game's creator can also keep them off.

The first time, the panel asks you to confirm: cheats change the game as its creator made it, and what they change stays in your saves. Save first (⚙ → **Quick save**) if you may want to go back; [older saves](/play/saves#bring-back-an-older-save) are a second safety net.

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Party** | **Heal everyone** (HP, MP, states). **God mode**: your party's HP never goes down and no one is knocked out; healing and MP work as usual. It stays on in this browser until you turn it off. **Level +1** for everyone below the top level. The **States** tab puts any state (poison, sleep, a buff…) on a party member or takes it off, found by name or number. The **Skills** tab teaches or forgets a skill, found by name, number or description (skills that come from the class or equipment stay) |
| **Gold and items** | Add any amount of gold. Browse items, key items, weapons and armor in their own tabs, or search all of them by name, number or description (you do not need to know the name: try "restores" or "cellar"), then add 1 or 10, up to the most the game lets you carry |
| **Battle** | **Win this battle**: every enemy falls and the battle ends as a normal victory, with EXP, gold and drops |
| **Switches and variables** | Search the game's switches and variables by the name its creator gave them, or by number, in two tabs, then turn a switch ON / OFF or type a variable's new value. Events read these to decide what happens, which helps when an event is stuck or a gallery room stays locked, but a wrong value can also block the story |
| **Movement** | **Walk through walls**: walls, events and the edges of the map no longer stop you; it stays on in this browser until you turn it off. **Remember this place** keeps up to 8 places; **Go** takes you back to one. The **Maps** tab searches a map by name or number and **Go** takes you to its middle |

Teleporting works on the map only, not in a menu, a battle or while an event is running. You land when the panel closes, with the game's usual fade; if you land inside a wall, turn on **Walk through walls** to get out.

Each section has its own tabs, and long lists show 40 rows at a time with a **Show more** button below.

The game pauses while the panel is open. Close it with **Close**, Esc or a tap outside it. The person who set up the game may have kept only some of the sections.
