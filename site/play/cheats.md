# Cheats

⚙ → **Cheats** opens a panel to change the game as you play. It is there only when the person who set up the game [turned cheats on](/setup/configuration#common-changes), for everyone or just for you; the game's creator can also keep them off.

The first time, the panel asks you to confirm: cheats change the game as its creator made it, and what they change stays in your saves. Save first (⚙ → **Quick save**) if you may want to go back; [older saves](/play/saves#bring-back-an-older-save) are a second safety net.

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Party** | **Heal everyone** (HP, MP, states). **God mode**: your party's HP never goes down and no one is knocked out; healing and MP work as usual. It stays on in this browser until you turn it off. **Level +1** for everyone below the top level |
| **Gold and items** | Add any amount of gold. Search items, weapons and armor by name and add 1 or 10, up to the most the game lets you carry |
| **Battle** | **Win this battle**: every enemy falls and the battle ends as a normal victory, with EXP, gold and drops |
| **Switches and variables** | Search the game's switches and variables by the name its creator gave them, or by number, then turn a switch ON / OFF or type a variable's new value. Events read these to decide what happens, which helps when an event is stuck or a gallery room stays locked, but a wrong value can also block the story |
| **Movement** | **Walk through walls**: walls, events and the edges of the map no longer stop you; it stays on in this browser until you turn it off. **Remember this place** keeps up to 8 places; **Go** takes you back to one. Search a map by name or number and **Go** to its middle |

Teleporting works on the map only, not in a menu, a battle or while an event is running. You land when the panel closes, with the game's usual fade; if you land inside a wall, turn on **Walk through walls** to get out.

The game pauses while the panel is open. Close it with **Close**, Esc or a tap outside it. The person who set up the game may have kept only some of the sections.
