# Open and play

This part is for players: someone sent you a link like `https://my-game.name.workers.dev`.

## Sign in

1. Open the link in a browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on a phone, tablet or PC.
2. Cloudflare asks for your **email**. Use the one the person who set up the game allowed.
3. Check your inbox for a one-time code, type it in, and the game loads.

No code arrives? Your email is probably not on the list yet: ask the person who set up the game to [add it](/setup/access#invite-players). Also check the spam folder.

::: tip Your email is your save
Your saves are tied to the email you sign in with. Use the same email on every device to continue your game. See [Saves on several devices](/play/saves).
:::

## Add it to the home screen

On a phone, the game plays best from the home screen: it opens full screen, like an app.

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| iPhone / iPad (Safari) | Share button → **Add to Home Screen** |
| Android (Chrome) | ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**) |

You can also tap ⛶ in the game's toolbar to go full screen. On a landscape game, this also turns the screen sideways where the browser allows it.

## Controls

| Device | How to play |
|---|---|
| Phone or tablet | The on-screen gamepad, see [Touch controls](/play/controls). Tapping the map to walk also works |
| PC | Keyboard (arrows, Enter / Z, Esc / X, Shift…) or a USB / Bluetooth controller, as in the desktop game |

## Tools

Tap ⚙ (in the toolbar on phones, a faint button in the top-left corner on a PC) for the toolkit's tools:

| Tool | Page |
|---|---|
| ✥ Edit button layout | [Touch controls](/play/controls#move-or-hide-buttons) |
| 💾 Saves | [Edit or move a save](/play/save-files) |

Some of these may be missing if the person who set up the game turned them off.
