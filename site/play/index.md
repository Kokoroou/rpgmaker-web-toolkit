# Open and play

Someone sent you a link like `https://my-game.name.workers.dev`.

1. Open it in any browser, on a phone, tablet or PC.
2. Type your **email**. It must be one the person who set up the game allowed.
3. Type the one-time code from your inbox. The game loads.

No code? Your email is not on the list yet (or the code went to spam). Ask for it to be [added](/setup/access#invite-players).

::: tip Your email is your save
Sign in with the same email everywhere to keep playing the same game. See [Saves on several devices](/play/saves).
:::

## Play full screen on a phone

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| iPhone / iPad (Safari) | Share → **Add to Home Screen** |
| Android (Chrome) | ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**) |

Or tap ⛶ in the game's toolbar.
